02 / 6:04 PM

The coach is coaching. The system should keep up.

Rally starts with the reality of the floor: twelve players, different needs, a short window and very little tolerance for admin friction.

01

Select people, not paperwork.

Start one drill for one player, a hand-picked group or the whole batch.

02

Effort is visible.

Coach rally time, multiball, drill sets and extra work become evidence rather than memory.

03

Coach decisions stay close.

The action queue only surfaces things that require judgment: approvals, readiness, entries and recommendations.

03 / PLAYER INTELLIGENCE

A coach should never start from zero with a player they already know.

Persistent ratings, comments, tournament readiness and focus history create an academy memory that can survive busy days, staff changes and growing batch sizes.

SKILL

Rate what matters

Forehand, backhand, movement, serve, receive, consistency and match temperament.

CONTEXT

Private coach memory

A score without a reason is weak. Attach comments and summaries to the skill.

READINESS

Evidence + human decision

Surface the evidence but keep tournament readiness controlled by the coach.

FOCUS

The week has a direction

Batch, group and individual planning follows the player into daily sessions.

REPORT

Progress is explainable

Attendance, match record, focus progress and coach feedback become one parent story.

NEXT

Every report points forward

The next focus is visible, not left as a vague “needs more practice.”

04 / COMPETITION

Training becomes more useful when competition belongs to the same system.

Rally treats internal and open tournaments as part of development: commercial rules, registration, categories, fixtures, live scores, results and readiness all connect back to the player.

01

Create

Internal/open, paid/free, early bird, categories, prize and format.

02

Register

Academy + external entries, payment state and eligibility.

03

Draw

Groups, fixtures, tables and schedule become one route.

04

Score

Live points, sets, timeout, correction and closure.

05

Learn

Results return to the same player identity and coaching plan.

05 / PLAYER + PARENT

Development should be felt between sessions, not only during them.

The player/parent side translates academy work into momentum: coach care, match preparation, badges, leaderboard, rewards, learning material and reports.

COACH CARE

“How is my child being developed?”

Show correction time, extra sets, personal feedback, clips and focus improvement with context.

MATCH PREP

Competition becomes a pathway.

Serve drills, opponent video, pressure simulations and coach approval can sit before the fixture.

MOTIVATION

Reward progress without trivialising it.

Badges, coins, streaks and challenges create memory around meaningful development milestones.

07 / WHO FEELS THE DIFFERENCE

The same system should make four different lives easier.

ACADEMY OWNER

See the academy as one operation.

Growth does not create more invisible coordination debt between coaches, tournaments and parents.

COACH

Remember more without typing more.

Floor actions create context that is available when rating, planning or reporting.

PLAYER

Know what progress feels like.

Skills, matches, badges, rewards and learning point toward the next development action.

PARENT

See coaching intent.

Not “attendance 88%.” What improved, why it matters and what comes next.

08 / HOW THE INVENTION FORMED

We did not begin with “academy management software.”

We began with the repeated moments that existing tools leave disconnected: a coach sees something, a player works on it, a match exposes it, a parent asks about it, and the academy has to reconstruct the story.

OBSERVE

The floor is alive.

Any system that slows coaching will be abandoned.

CONNECT

One player, many contexts.

Planning, training, matches and reports need the same identity.

RESPECT

Coaches keep judgment.

Readiness and feedback cannot be reduced to automation.

MOTIVATE

Progress needs memory.

Badges and rewards work when tied to verified effort.

OPERATE

The academy becomes coherent.

Admin supports sport instead of becoming a separate software universe.
