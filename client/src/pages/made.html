MADE BY THESIMPLE · THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE WORK

Talent does not
end at the job title.

This is our public wall for everything the team makes, notices, learns, cooks, paints, rides, photographs, repairs, writes or experiments with when nobody asked for a deliverable.

Enter the wall ↓

A wall that refuses to sit still.

Not a feed. Not employee engagement. Not “fun Friday.” Every object here is a small proof that someone on the team is interested enough in the world to make something outside their assigned work.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Curiosity is part
of the company.

Great product teams are built from people who notice textures, recipes, machines, streets, stories, colour, motion and strange little problems outside work. Made by thesimple makes that hidden range visible — to each other and to the world.
