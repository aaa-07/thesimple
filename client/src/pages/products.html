02 / BADMINTON COMPETITION ECOSYSTEM

AERYX

For organisers, academies and players who want the experience before, during and after a tournament to feel like one continuous competitive identity.

Explore AERYX ↗

The tournament should not become invisible the moment someone pays the entry fee or the moment the final shuttle lands.

BEFORE ENTRY

“Is this worth playing?”

Format, level, categories, capacity, prize context and meaningful competition cues help the player decide before registration.

AFTER THE EVENT

“What did this add to me?”

Match history, ratings, ranks, results, rewards and future opportunities keep competition alive after the venue empties.

⌕

Discover with confidence

Tournament information is structured around decision-making: format, audience, categories, entry, capacity and event character.

✓

Register without uncertainty

Entry becomes a guided flow tied to player identity instead of a form that loses context immediately after payment.

▦

Fixtures become a destination

Players can return to one place for schedule, court, group, knockout and event progression.

●

Live means live

Court calls, live fixtures, scores, set state and event updates belong together instead of scattered messages.

⌁

Academy matches matter too

Internal competition can carry ratings, identity and progression rather than feeling like disposable weekly play.

♜

Ratings create return

Open tournament and academy match ratings give players a reason to care about the next event, not only the current result.

◉

Rewards extend participation

Coins, store value, passes and incentives create useful activity between matches and events.

◎

Organisers gain one record

Entries, players/pairs, fixtures, scores, winners and event communication sit inside one operating layer.
